# Fieldnotes journal surface

## Scope and visitor mode

Redesign the journal index and article pages to follow the supplied interiors-
journal reference. Read: help readers discover and understand an editorial
note, then navigate back to the journal.

## Reader and task

Independent designers and design-minded readers arrive curious about ideas,
objects, and details shaping everyday life. They scan the latest writing, open
an essay, read comfortably, and return to the index.

## Content and constraints

Use the existing article and copy as the only published editorial content; do
not invent proof or replace editorial claims. Preserve static Astro routes,
the published-post filter, article metadata, the Fieldnotes name and required
palette, and working navigation. Guide, studio, and newsletter areas may exist
as clearly marked visual-only previews with no fake article links or active
email collection.

## Unresolved decisions

No external photography, testimonials, or additional published articles are
available. Keep all visual previews distinct from published content.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Make Fieldnotes feel like an independent design journal situated in
everyday spaces, with strong framing that always gives way to the essay.

OWN-WORLD: Borrow the reference's dark masthead, spacious two-part hero,
interior line art, open editorial grid, tall palette bars, and quiet signup
area. Translate its warm accent to Fieldnotes coral; retain Newsreader, DM Sans,
the required five-color palette, square geometry, and a warm-paper reading
surface.

STORY: Readers recognize the journal, find the newest real note and its subject
at a glance, follow it into a comfortable essay, and can return to the index at
any point. Clearly marked visual previews must never masquerade as published
articles or active services.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Keep Fieldnotes and the journal wayfinding in a dark teal
masthead. Place the existing two-part headline and copy beside a restrained
room illustration in a dark hero; the journal grid begins on warm paper below.
On an article route, put the return link, category, title, description, and
date in a dark frame before the calm reading column.

FORM: A journal in the room, adapted from the user-supplied reference. The
signature is a restrained room study in the hero, followed by spacious paper
and deep-teal color fields. A coral registration rail extends on a real
article link's hover or keyboard focus; reduced-motion preferences remove its
transition.

FINISH: Preserve the existing published-post filter, metadata, routes,
return-to-journal navigation, and reading-progress behavior. Verify the
homepage and article at desktop and mobile widths.
