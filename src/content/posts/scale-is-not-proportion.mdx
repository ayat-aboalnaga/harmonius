Proportion compares parts within a composition; scale compares size against a reference. Catalog photos often hide that reference, while a plan can make clearance look more generous than it feels at human height.

## Use a meaningful reference

Check furniture against people, doors, ceiling height, and circulation. Scale can feel intimate or monumental, but it should be a deliberate relationship to the room and its use.
