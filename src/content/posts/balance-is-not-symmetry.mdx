The idea persists because a centered elevation is easy to read and matching purchases are easy to source. But visual weight depends on size, color, contrast, and position, not sameness alone.

## Distribute visual weight

Balance is the sense of equilibrium created by the arrangement of forms. A large quiet piece can balance a small vivid one; an off-center composition can feel settled when its weights are thoughtfully distributed.
