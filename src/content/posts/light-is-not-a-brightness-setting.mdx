We inherit the idea that lighting is a fixture choice, rather than a relationship between source, surface, and activity. A single source can make a space technically bright yet visually harsh or functionally uneven.

## Let light do several jobs

Light reveals form, color, and texture; it also supports tasks and sets an atmosphere. Combine general illumination with focused task light and softer accents, while making use of daylight where it helps.
