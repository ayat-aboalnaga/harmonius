Showrooms make coordinated sets effortless to imagine, so “goes together” can start to mean “looks the same.” Yet harmony can hold difference when color, line, material, or mood provides a connecting thread.

## Find a common note

Harmony is compatibility among distinct elements. Repeat a warm tone or a gentle curve, then let other materials and forms vary so the room feels related rather than copied.
