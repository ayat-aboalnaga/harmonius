Paint catalogs encourage us to compare isolated swatches under showroom light. Color perception is relational: a surface changes in appearance beside another color, in shadow, and under different sources of light.

## Compose relationships

Consider hue, value (lightness or darkness), and intensity (muted or vivid) together. A restrained palette can still have contrast, and a small accent can feel bright because of what surrounds it.
