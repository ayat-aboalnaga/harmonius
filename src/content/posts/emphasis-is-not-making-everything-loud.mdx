The confusion comes from equating prominence with sheer intensity. If each element has the same contrast and volume, the eye has no clear hierarchy to follow.

## Give attention a place to land

Emphasis makes one part of a composition stand out through contrast, position, size, or detail. Let supporting elements stay quieter so the focal point feels intentional, not accidental.
