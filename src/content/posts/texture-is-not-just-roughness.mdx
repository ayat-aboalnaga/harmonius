The association comes from materials that announce themselves to the hand. But texture also reaches the eye: gloss, grain, weave, and shadow make surfaces look smooth, soft, or uneven from across the room.

## Read with hand and eye

Texture describes a surface quality as experienced by touch or suggested visually. Contrast a fine grain with a broad weave, or a matte plane with a small reflective detail; variety can be quiet.
