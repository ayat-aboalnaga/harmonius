The shortcut comes from elevation drawings and catalog images, where a complex object is reduced to a readable outline. But a profile cannot tell us how much space an object occupies, how it turns, or how it meets the room.

## Read the volume, not only the edge

Form is three-dimensional mass: its height, width, depth, and relationship to surrounding space. Look for how light, shadow, and viewpoint reveal a volume.
