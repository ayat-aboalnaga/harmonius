The mistake comes from treating proportion as a formula detached from use. Equal measurements may look arbitrary if they ignore what each part needs to do and how the whole is composed.

## Compare part to whole

Proportion is the comparative size of parts within an object, group, or space. Judge a relationship in context: a shade should relate to its base, and furniture should relate to the room and to neighboring pieces.
