This reading grows from the comfort of a single theme: it seems like a reliable shortcut to coherence. But uniformity can erase useful differences in scale, function, and character.

## Connect distinct parts

Unity is the perception that separate elements belong to one composition. Create it through a clear organizing idea, repeated alignments, or a limited set of relationships; allow the parts to keep their individual roles.
