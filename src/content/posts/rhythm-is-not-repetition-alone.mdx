The shortcut comes from familiar patterns such as repeated tiles or fixtures. Repetition is one tool, but without attention to spacing, change, or sequence, it may feel static rather than guide the eye.

## Shape a sequence

Rhythm is an organized movement created by recurring elements and intervals. Repetition, alternation, and gradual progression can each establish a beat that carries attention across a room.
