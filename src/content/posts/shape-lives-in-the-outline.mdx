That mix-up comes from everyday speech, where “shape” can describe nearly any visible outline. In design, the useful distinction is between a plane’s boundary and an object’s volume.

## Trace the bounded area

Shape is a two-dimensional figure defined by its outline or by a change in tone, color, or texture. In a room, notice both the shape of an object’s face and the shaped negative space around it.
