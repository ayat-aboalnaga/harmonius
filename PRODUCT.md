# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Astro

## Users

Independent designers and design-minded readers looking for considered writing
about design and its relationship to everyday life.

## Product Purpose

Fieldnotes is an independent journal about the ideas, objects, and details
shaping how people live. Its content helps readers look more closely at design
and its less-obvious effects on everyday experience.

## Positioning

The journal's distinctive positioning beyond its independent editorial focus
is not yet established.

## Operating Context

Readers browse the latest published notes, open an article, and return to the
journal. Editors add articles as Markdown or MDX posts, using reusable
components for annotated illustrations, definitions, and editorial callouts
when useful.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The journal listing and article routes are statically generated by default.
- Posts support a title, description, publication date, category, tags, and
  draft status.
- Draft posts are excluded from the public listing and generated article routes.
- Preserve existing article content and the listing, article, and return-to-
  journal navigation.

## Brand Commitments

- The product is named Fieldnotes.
- The redesign must use the supplied palette: #663732, #CC6D64, #64C3CC,
  #98C7CC, and #326166.

## Evidence on Hand

- One published article: `src/content/posts/designing-for-the-in-between.md`.
- No testimonials, customer claims, or other external proof are present.
